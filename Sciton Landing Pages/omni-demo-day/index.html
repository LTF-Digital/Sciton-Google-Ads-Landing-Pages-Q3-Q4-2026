Technology That Works Smarter.

OMNI™ isn’t just fast. It’s engineered for consistent, high-quality results with patient comfort in mind. Synergistic wavelengths, advanced cooling and customisable settings let you treat all skin types year-round with confidence.

True Wavelength Blend
Blended diodes create a homogeneous beam, while sapphire glass prevents particle burn-in and minimises maintenance.
Flat-top beam profile

Rectangular spot design
Covers more surface with fewer passes, eliminating overlap and reducing treatment time.
